      How the check works, and what it is worth

      A wallet calls one endpoint before an irreversible transfer settles. We return a score, a band and reasons; the wallet decides whether to warn, add friction, or let it through. Everything below is the real model on real held-out transfers.

      The dial a wallet turns

      Risk teams do not buy a score, they buy an operating point. Set the false-positive budget you can live with and the rest follows. Every number is measured on the held-out set, not modelled.

      What this looks like on real traffic

      the number that decides it

      Every other figure on this page is measured on a matched population where roughly one event in seven is a victim transfer. No wallet looks like that. Recall and false-positive rate do not move with prevalence — they are conditional on the true class — but precision does, and it is the thing a customer actually feels.

      So is it worth firing?

      Precision is the wrong lens. At a realistic prevalence most alerts are false — but a false alert costs a few seconds of friction and a true one stops an irreversible five-figure loss. The decision number is dollars protected per false alert; you supply the price of friction.

      What a flag is worth

      Nobody buys recall. A wallet trades money kept against customers interrupted, so this is the curve that matters. No cost per interruption is assumed — that price is the customer's — so it is reported as dollars protected per interruption and you can put your own number on friction.

      Measured on the held-out set. Dollars protected counts money that moved at or after our first flag on a victim relationship — money a blocking interstitial had the chance to stop.

      Does the model know what it knows?

      A score is only usable as a probability if it means what it says. Expected calibration error is the average gap between predicted and observed frequency across the score range.

      Calibration

      Stability out of period

      Population stability index between the train and test periods. Below 0.1 is stable, above 0.25 a material shift.
